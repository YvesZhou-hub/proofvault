Know the boundaries
  • A valid seal supports integrity checks after signing. It does not prove the original content is true.
  • Grounding measures matches against a limited register. Missing evidence is not proof of falsehood; incorrect source records can also mislead the check.
  • Bias requires contextual human judgement. The dashboard findings and suggested edits are pre-authored demonstration content.
  • Image, audio and video deepfake detection is outside this prototype. C2PA metadata is illustrative, not validated.
  • The API uses a local signed hash chain. Merkle trees and three-node consensus are browser-only concepts, not a deployed network. Server-managed demo keys do not authenticate reviewer identity.
