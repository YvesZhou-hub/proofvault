## Why（约 40 秒）· 契合度

Good morning, everyone. We are the team behind ProofVault.

A woman tells her doctor, "I take fifty milligrams of my heart medicine."

An AI tool is listening. But it writes five hundred milligrams.

That is ten times too much. And no one sees the mistake.

That wrong number is now in her health record. And records last. Years from now, someone will trust it as fact.

AI now writes records in hospitals, councils and offices. When it gets things wrong, misinformation and bias go straight into the archive.

---

## How（约 30 秒）· 创意与方案

The challenge asks: how can we build information management know-how into AI?

Our answer is ProofVault.

Records managers already know the rules. Every fact needs a source. Every change needs a history. And a real person makes the final decision.

ProofVault turns that know-how into a library of known AI mistakes, and checks every AI record against it before it is saved.
